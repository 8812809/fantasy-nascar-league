League Structure

The Fantasy NASCAR League is a 10-team private fantasy league. Each team carries 4 active NASCAR Cup Series cars during each segment.

  • 10 league teams
  • 4 cars per team
  • 3 scoring segments during the season
  • 12 races per segment
  • Full redraft after each segment

Team Rosters

Each team is responsible for the 4 cars drafted to its roster for the current segment. All points earned by those cars count toward that team's weekly score and segment standings while active.

Segment Format

The season is divided into 3 segments of 12 races each. At the end of each segment, standings are finalized and a full redraft is held.

Weekly Competition

Each team's weekly score is qualifying/start points plus race finish points earned by all 4 active cars. The highest weekly score wins the weekly side bet.

Scoring System

Qualifying / Starting Position Points

1st = 40 points, decreasing by 1 per position through 40th = 1 point.

Race Finish Points

1st = 120 points, decreasing by 3 per position through 40th = 3 points.

Driver Transactions

  1. Each team gets one car change per segment.
  2. No change until after Race 1 is completed.
  3. Changes must be completed before qualifying.
  4. Points already earned by the dropped car remain with the team.
  5. The new car earns points once active.
  6. After using the change, the team is locked until the next segment.

Official Results Source

Scoring is based on official NASCAR Cup Series qualifying/start information and official race results. Corrections may be posted when needed.
